👋 Meet the Team

Haii, Welcome to KoMo! 🌸

"KoMo — which stands for Cozy Moments — is a fun digital integrated game and website card play to help connect and keep in touch with your feelings."

We're a small team of teens who believe that every emotion deserves a safe, beautiful place to live. KoMo was born from late-night study sessions, heartfelt conversations, and a shared love for all things kozy 💜

Whether you're journaling a big feeling, snapping a silly photobooth moment, or just looking for a cozy corner of the internet — we made this space for you ✨
