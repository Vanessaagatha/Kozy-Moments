Questions? 🌸

Can I cancel my plan anytime?⌄
Yes, totally! You can cancel your plan at any time with no penalties. Your Premium features stay active until the end of your billing period 💜
What payment methods are accepted?⌄
We accept GoPay, OVO, QRIS, Virtual Account (BCA, BNI, Mandiri), and credit/debit cards. All payments are processed securely via Midtrans 🔒
Is my data safe and private?⌄
Absolutely! Your journal entries, photos, and memories are private and only visible to you. KoMo never sells or shares your personal data with anyone ✨
Can I switch between plans?⌄
Yes! You can upgrade or downgrade at any time. If you upgrade mid-cycle, you'll only be charged the difference. Downgrading takes effect at the start of your next billing period 🌸
Is there a student discount?⌄
KoMo is already priced for teens in mind! But if you're a student with a valid school email, DM us on Instagram for a special discount code 💜🎒
